Business Experience Meets Data Thinking

I love working with data, finding patterns, and turning numbers into meaningful insights. But what excites me just as much is sharing knowledge and helping people understand things they once found complicated.

Whether I am exploring a dataset, building a dashboard, or explaining a new concept, I believe the real value lies in making complex information simple, clear, and useful.
